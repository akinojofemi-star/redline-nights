Controls
← → A DSteer. The car accelerates by itself, but only you steer it: it turns as hard as you hold the key and runs straight when you let go, on bends and at forks alike. Steer into every bend yourself (the steering sensitivity is in the settings). ↓ SBrake. Tap brake while steering to start a drift, on a bend or a straight. Steer into it to tighten, steer the other way to swing into a drift the other way (chain them left-right down a straight), let go to ease out and the car runs straight. Nitro ends a drift, and starting one turns nitro off. Drifting charges nitro. ↓ ↓Double-tap brake for a 360 spin, on the road or in the air: it fills nitro and ends any nitro you're burning. Hit nitro during the spin to keep your speed up. Stack it with a barrel roll for a combo: purple corkscrew ramps always roll you, and clipping the edge of any other ramp with one side of the car tips you into one. Space ↑Nitro. One tap: yellow nitro, long and steady. Quick double tap: orange pulse, a hard push that works in the air but burns fast. Tap, then tap again in the blue zone: Perfect nitro, the highest top speed (it builds slowly), lasts longest and loses nothing in turns or on rough ground. Double-tap a full bar: Shockwave, the biggest punch for a second and a half, and nobody can knock you down. Once it's burning, more taps change nothing: nitro runs until the bar is empty, or until you drift or spin a 360. ForksWhere the road splits, the two roads overlap for a stretch: steer onto whichever one you want from any lane. Nothing steers you either way. The choice locks in at the striped barrier: arrive straddling it and you crash. The shortcut is shorter and tighter; the nitro route is longer but loaded with canisters, pads and ramps. On trackPurple corkscrew ramps add speed for as long as you're in the air. Parked cars and buses go flying when you hit them: on nitro it barely slows you, without it you lose a chunk of speed (more for a bus). Blue canisters add nitro. Green pads boost you for free. Barrels slow you; concrete roadblocks wreck you unless you're on nitro. CornersThe top of the screen calls out sharp bends and hairpins ahead. Take one faster than the tyres can hold and the car runs wide into the wall: brake in a straight line first, or drift through it. A drift corners harder than grip but scrubs off speed. The inside line is the shorter way round. EdgesWalls keep you on the road. Kerbed and cliff edges have a run-off with a red-and-white barrier at the end: run wide and you slow down on the verge, hit the barrier and you bounce off it like a wall. Only flying over it and landing beyond is out of bounds: you're put back where you left the road, a lot slower. Jumps over gaps need speed: come up short and you drop in. NitroFills from drifting, air time, near misses, knockdowns, stunts and slipstreams. The feed on the right counts drift distance and air time. CChange camera: chase (low, close), high chase, far chase, hood, bumper. P MPause, mute.
