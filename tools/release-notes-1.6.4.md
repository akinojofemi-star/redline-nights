Asphalt-style arcade street racer: race up to 6 drivers online (room codes) or over LAN, or play solo against AI.

**No install? Play in your browser:** https://akinojofemi-star.github.io/redline-nights/ (browser and app players can race each other).

## Download the app

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Redline.Nights.Setup.1.6.4.exe` (installer), or `Redline.Nights-1.6.4-win.zip` (no install: unzip, run *Redline Nights.exe*) |
| **Mac with Apple chip** (M1–M4) | `Redline.Nights-1.6.4-arm64.dmg` |
| **Intel Mac** | `Redline.Nights-1.6.4.dmg` |
| **Linux** | `Redline.Nights-1.6.4.AppImage` (ARM: `-arm64.AppImage`) |

Not sure which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" = arm64.

**Everyone needs 1.6.x (or the browser version) to play together.** Older copies show a version-mismatch message.

## First launch
The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so you'll get a one-time warning:
- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC" → **More info** → **Run anyway**. When hosting a LAN game, allow it through the firewall (Private networks).
- **Mac:** open the .dmg and drag the app to Applications. When macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** → **Open Anyway**. If it says the app "is damaged", run `xattr -cr "/Applications/Redline Nights.app"` in Terminal.
- **Linux:** `chmod +x Redline.Nights-*.AppImage`, then run it.

## What's new in 1.6.4
- **You do all the steering:** the car no longer turns itself into bends or toward a fork. It turns as hard as you steer and runs straight when you let go. Drifts turn as hard as you steer into them.
- **Nitro stays on:** once it's burning, extra taps change nothing. It only ends when the bar is empty, or when you drift or spin a 360. A late second tap gives the orange pulse.
- **Perfect nitro timing bar:** a big bar in the middle of the screen; the window lights up, and on phones the nitro button glows, while the tap counts.
- **Wider roads:** narrow sections are wider and every tight bend opens out.
- **Nothing to drive through:** gantry legs, billboards, signs, trees, rocks and palms that stood on a road or run-off have moved out of the way. On three forks the divider sat in the middle of the road; it's back at the real split. Ramps are solid from the side.
- **Clearer roads:** darker tarmac and different-coloured run-off and ground on Neon City, Tokyo, Magma Rift, Canyon and Emerald Ruins.

## What's new since 1.3.0
- **Looks:** photo-scanned road, wall, building and terrain textures on every map; real facades with lit windows at night; hills and mountains on the open-country maps; all car models rebuilt with more detail and their real two-tone paint, chrome and carbon
- **43 cars**, including the Chiron, Rimac Nevera, LaFerrari, McLaren P1 and Senna, Aventador SVJ, Ford GT, Ferrari F8 and 812, 911 Turbo S, NSX, Challenger Hellcat, Camaro ZL1, Focus RS, M2, RX-7 and 370Z; classes rebalanced
- **Four nitros:** yellow (tap), orange pulse (quick double tap), blue Perfect (highest top speed, lasts longest) and Shockwave (full bar)
- **Asphalt-style camera**, crisper steering and a steering-sensitivity setting
- **Chain drifts:** steer the other way mid-drift to swing into a drift the other way
- **Slipstream trails** you can see and follow
- **Fair contact online:** takedowns need nitro and intent; bumping never wrecks you
- **Forks:** choose either road right up to the split; arriving late hits the divider
- **Track fixes:** solid barriers at the edge of every run-off, jumps that land with room to see the next bend, the Industrial Surge tunnel fixed, nitro canisters always collected
- **Scenery:** photographed real trees (firs, jacarandas, quiver trees) and real boulders from CC0 Poly Haven models; textured trees, temples, rocks and ice elsewhere
- **Phones:** gauge at the top, compact layouts for portrait and landscape

## Controls
Steer ←/→ or A/D (the car accelerates itself) · Brake ↓/S: tap while steering to drift · Double-tap ↓ for a 360 · Nitro Space/↑ · Camera C · Mute M · Fullscreen F11
